## Parking Plot Design 
### Requirements


1. **Big Parking Lot**
   Size: 10,000 to 30,000 parking spots
   Adequate space for various types of vehicles
2. **Entrances and Exits**
   4 entrances and 4 exits for efficient traffic flow
   Clearly marked entry and exit points 
3. **Ticketing System**
   Automated ticketing system at entrances
   Parking spots assigned upon entry
   Integration with spot availability monitoring system
4. **Proximity to Entrance**
   Parking spots allocated based on proximity to entrances
   Priority given to handicapped, compact cars, large cars, and motorcycles
5. **Capacity Limit**
   System should prevent entry once capacity reaches 30,000
   Real-time monitoring of parking lot occupancy
6. **Parking Spot Variety**
   Designated spots for handicapped, compact cars, large cars, and motorcycles
   Clearly marked and easily accessible spots
7. **Hourly Rate**
   Hourly parking rate for all types of vehicles
   Payment options for hourly parking
8. **Payment Options**
   Cash payment facilities at exits
   Credit card payment terminals at exits
   UPI payment integration for online transactions
9. **Monitoring System**
   CCTV cameras for monitoring parking lot activity
   Automated system alerts for any irregularities or issues
10. **Scalability**
    Design a flexible system that can be easily deployed in multiple locations with minimal modifications
    Centralized management system for all parking lots
